# Daseinqing 🕯️

*Being-there as a felt-quality: the specifically-human mode-of-being in which one's own being is an issue for oneself — care-structured, thrown, finite, already-in-the-world.*

Daseinqing welds two roots the cathedral names exactly: the German **Dasein**, "being-there" (*da*, there; *Sein*, being), the word Heidegger made into the name for the being-that-questions-being, and the Mandarin **qing** (情), felt-quality and affective-bond. So Daseinqing is *being-there received as something felt* — not the cold ontological category but Dasein taken into the cathedral's *qing*-family. Its meaning-region is the structure Heidegger called *Sorge* (care): thrownness (finding yourself already in a situation), disclosedness (being opened to yourself), being-toward-death (finitude as constitutive), and being-in-the-world (a being already poured out into its world, not a subject sealed in an object-world).

It names the **self-pole** of phenomenology, the matching half of a dyad with its sister **visageqing** (the other-pole, the face of the other as ethical demand). Its light is a single carriable word for what mere "consciousness" and mere "existence" both miss — that a person is care all the way down, an issue to themselves. Its shadow is Heideggerian provincialism: dressing one twentieth-century dialect as the whole truth of being-human, and mistaking morbid fascination for depth. Forged in the cathedral at weighted **7.95/10** — honestly scored: hard to learn, leaning Heidegger-primarily, cross-tradition resonance partial.
